03 // THE 4-PERSON CAMPUS CHAPTER POD & SUCCESSION

Lean Pod Roles, Hard Weekly KPIs & Zero-Collapse Continuity

A chapter never needs 20 club members sitting in meetings. It runs on 4 paid operators working 8-12 focused hours/week, paired with a semester-by-semester shadow bench so graduation never breaks a client account.

SEAT 01 // BD & CLOSING 10-12 hrs/wk

Chapter Managing Director

Owns local prospecting, off-peak walk-ins, 15-min discovery calls, closing Sprints/Retainers, client Day-10 recaps, and running the weekly standup.

  • KPI: 25+ local touches / wk
  • KPI: 3+ discovery calls / wk
  • KPI: >= 85% 90-day retention
  • Split: 28% pod pool + 15% closer fee
SEAT 02 // CREATIVE 8-10 hrs/wk

Signal Lead

Owns shoot pre-production, 3-second opening hooks, on-site directing, interviewing owners/students, and maintaining crisp L/I wit + warmth tone.

  • KPI: Briefs locked 48h pre-shoot
  • KPI: >= 38% 3-sec hold rate
  • KPI: 2 clean shoots / client / mo
  • Split: 24% of pod pool
SEAT 03 // PRODUCTION 10-12 hrs/wk

Wildfire Editor

Owns ingest, pacing, kinetic captions, audio polish, multi-format fracture, and rapid cut turnaround using Central Studio templates and vault presets.

  • KPI: 12-24 cuts shipped / wk
  • KPI: 100% inside 72h edit SLA
  • KPI: Asset vault tagged daily
  • Split: 26% of pod pool
SEAT 04 // DISTRIBUTION 8-10 hrs/wk

Live Rooms Producer

Owns vetted roster of 15-30 local campus micro-creators, club partnerships, street-level QR activations, and monthly Live Room pop-up events.

  • KPI: 4 creator collabs / client / mo
  • KPI: 1 Live Room or pop-up / mo
  • KPI: 50+ tracked QR scans / drop
  • Split: 22% of pod pool
Semester Continuity Protocol :: Anti-Graduation-Cliff Architecture 002 CHARCOAL
STAGE 01 // SOPHOMORE SHADOW
Paid Per-Shoot Apprentice

Every chapter keeps 2-4 Sophomore Shadows on deck. Shadows attend 2 shoots, 2 discovery calls, and edit 4 backup cuts per semester (paid $75-$150/project from pod reserve) so they know the playbook cold before taking a seat.

STAGE 02 // JUNIOR LEAD
Active 4-Seat Operator

Holds one of the 4 core pod seats for two consecutive semesters. Owns full client SLA, earns core pod revenue share, and begins mentoring their own Sophomore Shadow by Week 6 of their second term.

STAGE 03 // SENIOR ADVISOR
Clean Handoff + Regional Seed

Hands over day-to-day seat by Week 10 of spring semester, earns a 5% trailing handoff bonus for 90 days when accounts stay retained, and focuses on regional $6.8k Syndicate deals or seeding neighboring campuses.

SHARED OPERATING BACKBONE
Centralized CRM + Asset Vault

No chapter stores client history in personal DMs or laptops. Every lead lives in the Central CRM (Stages: Prospect → Walk-In/DM → Discovery → Sprint → Retainer → Renewal). Every shoot uploads raw footage, caption templates, and QR analytics into the Shared Asset Vault within 12 hours of wrap so any editor or incoming Junior Lead can step in seamlessly.
